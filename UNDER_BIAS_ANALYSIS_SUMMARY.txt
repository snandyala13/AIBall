WHY DOES THE UNDER BIAS EXIST?
================================================================================

ROOT CAUSE: Distributional, not a pricing error

Key Insight:
  - DK sets lines at outcome MEDIANS (correct pricing)
  - But actual outcomes are RIGHT-SKEWED (skew = +0.254)
  - Result: 52% of outcomes fall below the median line
  - But rare big wins pull the mean back up to line value

Evidence:
  - Mean actual: 10.744
  - Mean line: 10.669
  - Median diff: -0.500 (median outcome = line - 0.5)
  - Percentiles show left tail is denser than right tail

Why DK's Model is Too High:
  - When DK projects > line: only 50.40% come in under
  - When DK projects < line: 53.80% come in under
  - Difference: 3.40 percentage points
  - This suggests DK's projection model OVERESTIMATES

Vigorish Insight:
  - Threes have +2.64% vig skew toward unders (unusual!)
  - Overall vig: 6.46%
  - DK shades 0.67% more vig to under side
  - This hints DK knows about the bias
